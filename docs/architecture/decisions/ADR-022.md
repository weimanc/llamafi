### ADR-022 — 2026-05-17 — DUT–Spotify state synchronization lag-bound contract (M-SYNC)

**Status**: accepted (2026-05-17)

**Context**:

M-SYNC (sync-001) tests measure how long it takes for a Spotify-side state change to
propagate to the DUT's chrome render. Without a formal lag-bound contract the tests
have no pass/fail criterion — a 30-second lag is technically "eventually correct."

Three distinct propagation regimes exist, each with different expected timing:

1. **Steady-state** — Spotify mutates; DUT is idle, polling normally.
2. **Degraded** — DUT is in exponential back-off due to consecutive failures.
3. **Touch-confirmed** — DUT initiated the change via touch (volume, transport, shuffle,
   repeat); optimistic-UI freeze holds the rendered value while waiting for confirmation.

The lag in each regime is dominated by a different mechanism:

- Steady-state: one poll cycle (base 5 s) + render latency.
- Degraded: remaining back-off window + one poll round-trip.
- Touch-confirmed: optimistic-hold window + one poll.

Additionally, **cross-poll transients** (e.g. track A → B → A within one poll window)
are a legitimate edge case where the DUT may legally miss the intermediate state —
final convergence is the contract, not per-transition observability.

---

**Decision**

**1. Three-tier lag-bound contract**

| Regime | Bound | Derivation |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Steady-state field change (Spotify-side, DUT idle) | **5 500 ms** | base poll 5 000 ms + 500 ms render latency |
| Degraded (DUT in back-off, `consecutiveFailures ≥ 1`) | **backoff_remaining_ms + 1 000 ms** (max 61 000 ms) | io-001 ladder max 60 s + one poll round-trip |
| Touch-confirmed field (volume, transport, seek with optimistic freeze) | **optimistic_window_ms + 5 500 ms** (≤ 9 000 ms) | hold window 1 500–2 000 ms + steady-state bound |

Bounds apply from `t_send` (host-side action timestamp) to `t_seen` (first DUT `get snapshot`
or heartbeat log showing the new value).

**2. Cross-poll transient rule**

The DUT may legitimately miss intermediate states when multiple Spotify mutations arrive
inside one poll window. The contract is: *final state correct within one poll cycle of
the last mutation*, not *all intermediate states observed*. T108 (TSYNC-12) is the
designated test for this; it is classified `manual log scrape` until a firmware
`LOG_W("spotify.poll", "track transition skipped ...")` guard is in tree.

**3. Test failure criterion**

A test fails if:
- The DUT snapshot does not reflect the Spotify mutation within the stated bound, OR
- The DUT reports an intermediate incorrect value *after* the bound has elapsed, OR
- The DUT misreports a stable state (no mutation in flight) — no bound, always a failure.

A test is **inconclusive** (not a failure) if the DUT was in degraded mode at `t_send`
and `backoff_remaining_ms` is unknown — retry after `set backoff 0` + `reconnect`.

**4. Observability prerequisites**

Tests assert the bound using:
- `get snapshot.lastPollAgeMs` (SERIALDBG-l) — the age of the snapshot at the time of
  assertion. If `lastPollAgeMs > 5 500 ms`, the DUT has not polled since the mutation
  was sent; extend the wait and retry.
- Heartbeat `last_poll_age_ms` + `next_poll_in_ms` (TASK-058) — passive monitoring
  without serial commands; cheaper during long waits.
- `tools/tsync_diff.py` (TASK-057) — per-field comparison; anchor for T110 (TSYNC-14).

These prereqs gate suite execution; see sync-001 test plan preamble.

**5. Out of scope**

- Sub-poll interpolation accuracy (position bar between polls) — this is poll-002's
  remit (M4 interpolation tests).
- Audio-level synchronization — not a feature of this firmware.
- Spotify Free account behaviour — contract assumes Spotify Premium.

---

**Alternatives considered:**

**Single flat bound (e.g. 10 s for all cases)**: Too loose for steady-state (masks
regressions), too tight for degraded (produces false failures during back-off recovery).
Rejected.

**Per-field bounds**: Unnecessary complexity — all fields share the same poll mechanism.
Volume is the only outlier (optimistic hold); captured in the touch-confirmed tier.

**No formal contract (ad-hoc per test)**: Already the status quo; makes T097–T110
pass/fail criteria ambiguous. Rejected.

---

**Consequences:**

What becomes easier:
- sync-001 tests have unambiguous pass/fail — just compare elapsed time to the bound.
- Degraded-regime tests (T105) don't need a fixed timeout; they compute the bound from
  the observed `consecutiveFailures` state via `get backoff`.
- Future regression: any poll-cycle change (cadence, back-off ladder) requires an
  ADR-022 amendment to update the bounds — deliberate coupling.

What becomes harder:
- Tests must capture `lastPollAgeMs` or the heartbeat to confirm a poll occurred;
  wall-clock elapsed time alone is insufficient.
- Cross-poll transient tests (T108) remain manual until the firmware LOG_W guard lands.
