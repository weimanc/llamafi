### IFC-006 — display / renderer seam  *(RESERVED — NOT YET CONTRACTED)*

**Status**: proposed
**As-built**: Number reserved; contract not written.

**Parties**: all renderers ↔ the `TFT_eSPI tft` global
**Transport**: direct method calls on a global object

**Why it must exist.** 797 `tft.` call sites across 24 files, against an object currently *defined in
vendored upstream compatibility code* (`cheapYellowLCD.h:7`) that nobody owns. ADR-061 D9 step 1
makes it an owned component (`display/tft.{h,cpp}`); this contract states what callers may assume
once it is.

**What it must cover**: the `startWrite()`/`endWrite()` batching discipline two renderers already
share (X005); who may call (IFC-002 I2 — `loopTask` only); viewport/clip restoration obligations
(`util/tftViewportRepair.h` exists because this was got wrong); and the RGB565 colour convention.

**Note on scope.** This is a *contract over the existing global*, not a canvas abstraction layer.
ADR-028 rejected the latter and that rejection stands — though its reasoning predates both the
component model and the host-unit-test possibility, so it is worth a re-read (M-SRCLAYOUT §"what's
left", item 6).

**Blocked on**: ADR-061 D9 step 1 (TASK-468).

**Version**: none — stub
