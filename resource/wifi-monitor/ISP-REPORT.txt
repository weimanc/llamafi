Subject: Velop MX5600 - 2.4 GHz radio keeps dropping out (intermittent, worst in the evenings)

Hi,

I have a recurring fault with my Linksys Velop MX5600 (model MX56HF, firmware
1.0.2.216845, serial 66H10M22F02460). The 2.4 GHz radio goes off the air for a
few seconds at a time, over and over. It is fine overnight but gets steadily
worse through the day and is at its worst in the evening. Phones and other
2.4 GHz devices keep dropping off the wifi because of it. The 5 GHz radio is not
affected.

Before contacting you I did some testing to rule out my own equipment. From a
laptop a few metres from the router I ran repeated wifi scans (Linux, nmcli and
iw) every couple of seconds and logged whether my 2.4 GHz network was visible.
In the same scan I also logged how many neighbouring networks were visible, as a
control. Every time my network disappeared, the neighbours' networks were still
there - so the scan itself was working and the outage was my router, not my
laptop or interference. I saw the same drops independently from a second device
(a small ESP32 board) that logged the same timeouts.

I logged this over several days (10 to 12 July) and it repeats the same way
every day: quiet overnight, worse through the day, and worst in the evening.
On 10 July alone I recorded 173 dropouts in about 20 hours. Rough rate by time
of day:

  overnight (02:00-07:00):  about 1 every 90 minutes
  daytime   (09:00-11:00):  about 1 every 5-8 minutes
  evening   (18:00-21:00):  about 1 every 2 minutes (peak 37 in one hour)

Each dropout lasts a few seconds up to about half a minute (longest was ~28 s).
Signal was strong the whole time (around -45 dBm), so this is not a range or
coverage problem - the radio just stops transmitting for a few seconds and comes
back. It is worst when the band is busiest (evenings), but the neighbouring
networks stay visible right through each drop, so it is my radio going quiet,
not general interference knocking everything out.

I have already tried the obvious fixes without success: rebooted the router,
pinned both the 2.4 and 5 GHz radios to fixed channels (so it is not auto-channel
switching), and split the 2.4 GHz band onto its own SSID. These reduced the
frequency but did not stop it.

This looks like a firmware or hardware fault in the 2.4 GHz radio. Could you tell
me whether this is a known issue on this model/firmware and whether there is a
firmware update, or whether the unit needs replacing?

I have the full timestamped logs if they are useful - the per-scan capture from
the laptop (presence of my network vs neighbours, every ~2.5 s) and the capture
from the second device. Happy to send them over.
